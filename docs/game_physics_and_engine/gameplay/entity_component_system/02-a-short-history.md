# A short history

Why do Unity, Unreal and Bevy all ship nearly the same entity model, built years apart? Because the component pattern predates the term "ECS" by over a decade, and the engines converged on ideas published and shipped between 2002 and 2022.
Timeline: from Dungeon Siege (2002) to Unreal's Mass Entity (2022)

Scott Bilas's component-based objects for Dungeon Siege (2002)[1] came first; Adam Martin's 2007 blog series named the pattern and its strict data/logic split[3]; and between 2017 and 2022 it shipped in EnTT, Overwatch, Unity, Flecs, Bevy and Unreal's Mass[12][7][23].
