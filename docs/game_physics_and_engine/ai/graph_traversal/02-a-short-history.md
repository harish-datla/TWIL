# A short history

Why does a shortest-path algorithm published in 1959 still decide how game characters walk? Graph theory predates computing by two centuries (Euler, Seven Bridges of Königsberg, 1736), and graph algorithms arrived with electromechanical switching and didn't slow down.
Timeline: from 1882 to jump point search (2011)

The engine-relevant line runs from Moore's BFS (1957)[7] and Dijkstra's shortest paths (1959)[9], through Kahn's topological sort (1962)[12] and A* (1968)[13], to Recast and Detour (2009)[2] and jump point search (2011)[20].
