# A short history

Why does a 1993 shooter's level format contain a tree, and why do today's ray-tracing GPUs walk one in hardware? Spatial data structures emerged from three separate communities: computer graphics (visibility), computational geometry (range searching), and database indexing (spatial queries).
Timeline: from BSP trees (1980) to Box2D's dynamic BVH (2019)

The engine-relevant milestones: BSP trees (1980)[2] and Doom's use of them (1993)[7], octrees (1982)[1], automatic BVH construction with surface area as its cost (1987)[5], loose octrees (2000)[8], binned SAH fast enough for per-frame rebuilds (2007)[9], and BVH traversal in GPU hardware (2018)[19].
