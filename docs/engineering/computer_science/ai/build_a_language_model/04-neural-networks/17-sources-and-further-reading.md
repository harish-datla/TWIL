A note on originality

The prose in this tutorial is original writing. The mathematical equations themselves are canonical statements that appear identically across every neural-network textbook (there's only one way to write the sigmoid function or the four backpropagation equations), and they are attributed at the points where they are introduced. The labeling convention BP1–BP4 follows Nielsen [4], who derives all four in detail. The scaled dot-product attention formula appears verbatim in Vaswani et al. [9]. The interactive demos, code, and CSS on this page are written from scratch for this tutorial.
